About JF

A small start that became a real passion.

JF Auto Detailing is a young, owner-led detailing business based in Marks Tey.

Founded in 2024

It started with a holiday goal.

The owner started cleaning cars at 14 to earn money for a holiday with his grandad. What began as a practical way to save quickly turned into a genuine passion for car care.

As the work developed, that interest grew into JF Auto Detailing: a focused drop-off service offering valeting, deep interior cleaning, paint decontamination, machine polishing and ceramic coating.

The most rewarding part is taking a dirty car and returning it looking, feeling and smelling fresh — then seeing the customer’s reaction to the finished result.

The approach

Straightforward, careful and ambitious.

The business is still growing, but the standard is simple: offer only the services JF actually performs and give every vehicle the attention it needs.
