Clear answers about the current JF Auto Detailing booking and drop-off process.

Call, text or use the booking form. JF will reply and agree a suitable date and time directly with you.

JF currently uses a drop-off and collection system in Marks Tey. The exact arrangement is confirmed when you book.

Working times are flexible and arranged to suit the customer where possible.

Most services do not require a deposit. Machine polishing and ceramic coating require a 50% deposit.

Payment is accepted by cash or bank transfer.

The booking can be rearranged for a more suitable day.

Access to a hose tap and an electrical plug for the extension cable is required.

The time depends on the service and the condition of the vehicle. JF will discuss this when arranging the booking.
