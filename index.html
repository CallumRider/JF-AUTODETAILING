The story behind JF

Started at 14. Built through genuine passion.

JF Auto Detailing began in 2024 when the owner started cleaning cars to earn money for a holiday with his grandad. That first goal led to a genuine interest in car care, and the work has since grown into a detailing business based in Marks Tey.

The part he enjoys most is taking a dirty car and returning it looking, feeling and smelling fresh — then seeing the customer’s reaction.
