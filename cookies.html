Cookies and third-party content

What this website stores, when the map connects to Google, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 2 August 2026
On this pageCurrent cookie use Interactive maps Hosting and technical logs Your browser controls Future changes

Current cookie use

JF Auto Detailing does not currently use advertising cookies, analytics cookies or personalised tracking on this website. The site does not include a cookie-consent banner because it does not intentionally place optional cookies itself.

Interactive maps

The Google map is blocked until you actively select “Load map”. Loading it connects your browser to Google Maps. Google may then use cookies, local storage or similar technologies under its own terms and privacy information.

You can avoid that connection by leaving the map unloaded and using the written Marks Tey location information instead.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process limited technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to deliver and secure the website. JF does not use those logs to build advertising profiles.

Your browser controls

You can remove or block cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all storage may affect third-party features that you choose to load.

Future changes

If analytics, advertising tools or other optional technologies are introduced, this page and the privacy notice must be updated and an appropriate consent choice will be added before those technologies load.
